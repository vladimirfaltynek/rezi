Frequently asked questions

How do I reach the Second Year?

The Second Year opens after you finish level 20. A “Second Year” tab then appears on the map.

Where are the Christmas levels?

The Christmas Forest (level 22) opens after level 20, Christmas with Santa (level 44) after level 42.

Is my progress saved across devices?

Yes. Progress syncs through iCloud between your iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.

The game is too hard for my child.

Turn on Assist Mode in Settings: more health leaves, a slower game and no losing.

Can I play with a game controller?

Yes, the game supports PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo controllers, and the keyboard on Mac.

Privacy policy

Rézi the Squirrel does not collect any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to the developer or to third parties.

Your game progress is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, it syncs between your devices through iCloud; this data is managed by Apple and the developer has no access to it.

Achievements and leaderboards use Apple’s Game Center when you are signed in. This data is also managed by Apple under its privacy policy.

The game has no in-app purchases, no chat and no links to social networks.
