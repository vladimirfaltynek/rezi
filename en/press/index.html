About the game

A storm has scattered the squirrels’ winter stores all over the forest, and little Rézi has to gather them before the snow comes. Across 44 levels she travels through the spring, summer, autumn and winter of two whole years – jumping, climbing, gliding on her bushy tail and meeting the animals of the forest.

Boss fights are won by helping, not hurting: Rézi plugs holes in a beaver dam, returns stolen things and tucks a sleepy bear back in. Every level has its own painted postcard and fairy tale, and a living storybook tells the story between the seasons.

New in version 1.2: a complete Second Year (20 new levels with a story about a lost fawn), two Christmas levels including the big “Christmas with Santa” level, and the secret Magpie’s Nest.

  • no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection
  • assist mode for the youngest players, larger text, no-flash mode
  • game controllers, customisable touch controls, VoiceOver in menus
  • Game Center (26 achievements) and iCloud progress sync
